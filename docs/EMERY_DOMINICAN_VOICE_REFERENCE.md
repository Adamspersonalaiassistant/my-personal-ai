## Linguistic guidance

Dominican Spanish commonly has fast/lively rhythm and Caribbean intonation, with weakening or aspiration of syllable-final /s/ in Spanish. These facts should inform **rhythm and subtle vocal color**, not be copied mechanically into English.

For English previews:
- preserve full intelligibility
- retain subtle Spanish-first vowel color in selected words
- use lively Caribbean rhythm and expressive pitch movement
- allow occasional gentle terminal-consonant softening only where natural
- do not mechanically delete English plural -s
- do not roll every English /r/
- do not fake grammar errors or broken English
- do not exaggerate a stereotype
